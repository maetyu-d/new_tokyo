YORU / TOKYO 1.14.0 — SHARED FLOW GEOMETRY

WORK REDUCTION
Each animated strand corner previously recalculated its two bends, segment
endpoints and normalized edge direction. The vertex shader now fetches these
from a full-precision table shared by link length, seventeen phase classes, four
strands and segment index. Its algebra preserves the original per-segment edges;
no strands, segments, particles or contacts are removed. The table contains 816
samples in the dense overview and 1,360 at the crown/pan settings, with one upload
per changed process time. Identical paused time performs no calculation or upload.
The table is bounded by the same finite strand/phase/LOD vocabulary. This fixture
uses two length variants and at most 21,760 bytes of float shape data.

Power, water and rail views have separate index lists over the same vertex VBOs.
Only the chosen list is submitted. The lists partition the original index list
and retain each layer's ordering. The main view uses the untouched combined index
order. The vertex shader no longer decodes visibility to discard hidden geometry;
CPU chunk/floor culling and filtered index lists remove it before processing.

entries in total; they do not duplicate position, colour, normal or UV data.

DETERMINISTIC WORK REDUCTIONS
The full skyline submits 1,278,472 network vertices instead of 1,917,708: exactly
one third fewer. The indexed triangles preserve the original quads and draw order.
A fifteen-entry curve-basis uniform and seventeen sine/cosine phase pairs replace
millions of repeated per-vertex trigonometric evaluations. Mesh vertex bytes also
fall by one third; no additional per-vertex coefficient attributes are retained.

Stationary ground and structure surfaces are reused, with selective invalidation
for material/support/junction/view changes. The optical neighbourhood is cached
in full-precision float colour targets. Live feedback, time, traffic, cabins,
walking signals and building motion continue independently. During camera motion,
direct scene drawing and the original direct field shader avoid cache rebuild
passes. Warm fixed-view samples contain no surface or field rebuilds; panning
samples use the direct path. All 2D targets omit unused depth attachments.
Unfocused interactive windows cap at 15 FPS; focused windows retain the 60 FPS
cap. The explicit benchmark runs uncapped regardless of focus.
