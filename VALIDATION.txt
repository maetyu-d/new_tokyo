RULES AND SCOPE
This is a fictional game model. Abstract bearing weights/capacities, head by
stratum, mobility shares, appointments, travel budgets and health evolution are
planning rules, not real engineering, medical or population estimates. Loads
use shortest bearing paths with equal splits, not finite-element analysis.
Water uses bounded head and quantity dispatch, not hydraulic pressure dynamics.
People remain routed batches; fire egress, lift scheduling, private-access hours
and ownership law are not simulated. No win/lose condition was introduced.
The Afterimage machine view and Aftertaste floor diagram edit the same city.
Original Aftertaste and Afterimage projects remain untouched.
